## Why this topic is coming up

> **Search is turning into answers — and our HTML is still the source.**

More and more searches end without a click: Google's AI Overviews, ChatGPT, and
Perplexity read the web and *answer* directly. That reshuffles SEO (Search Engine Optimization) into two new
acronyms — **AEO (Answer Engine Optimization)** and **GEO (Generative Engine
Optimization)** — the craft of being the source an AI quotes. It builds straight
on our [SEO](seo-structured-data) lesson, and it is squarely dev work: clean,
crawlable, well-structured content is what these systems can actually read.

## What we will cover

- **What AEO and GEO actually mean**, and how they differ from classic ranking.
- **Why the fundamentals matter *more*, not less.** Google's own position is
  that there are no special tricks for AI Overviews — indexable pages, real
  content, structured data, and speed still decide it (see the "Go deeper" link).
- **Structured data as machine-readable meaning** — the same
  [schema markup](seo-structured-data) that earns rich results also helps
  machines quote you accurately.
- **`llms.txt`** — an emerging (not yet universal) convention for handing AI
  models a clean Markdown map of your site, a cousin of `robots.txt`.
- **Measuring it** — spotting AI assistants in referral traffic, and knowing
  what is and is not trackable yet.

## Until the session

1. Ask an AI assistant a question your project's site should be the answer to.
   Does it cite the site? Someone else? Nobody?
2. Check whether a site you work on even *has* a `robots.txt` and structured
   data — the foundations AEO builds on. Would a machine understand your top page?
