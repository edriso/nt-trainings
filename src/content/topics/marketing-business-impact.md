## Why this topic is coming up

> **Every ticket we ship is someone's marketing dollar at work.**

Clients rarely ask "is the code clean?". They ask *"I spend $10k a month on
Facebook and Google — which one is working?"* and *"was this feature worth
building?"*. This session is about answering those questions with a straight
face.

## What we will cover

- **The channels** — paid search, paid social, organic search, email, direct —
  and what each one costs (CPC — cost per click — and friends).
- **The acronyms of money** — ROAS (return on ad spend), CAC (customer
  acquisition cost), AOV (average order value) — in plain English.
- **Attribution** — deciding which channel "gets credit" for a sale, and why
  every tool reports a different answer (this builds directly on the
  [Google Analytics 4](google-analytics-4) lesson).
- **Making a business case** — turning "we improved LCP by 40%" into a sentence
  a CEO cares about, with numbers.

## Until the session

1. In the [GA4 demo account](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/6367342), open Acquisition and compare "Paid Search" against "Organic Search" — which drives more revenue per session?
2. Take a ticket you shipped recently and try to write its one-sentence business case: *who* it affects, and *what number* it should move.
